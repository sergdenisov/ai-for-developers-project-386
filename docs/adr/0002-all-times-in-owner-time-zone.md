# All times shown in the Owner Time Zone

Working Hours, the Booking Window, Slots and Bookings are defined and shown in one Owner Time Zone (Moscow time), not
in each Guest's local time; the zone is labelled wherever times appear. Converting to the Guest's zone would shift
Slots across day boundaries in the calendar and make "today" ambiguous, which is not worth it for a single-Owner
learning project. A Guest in another zone therefore sees Moscow time on purpose.
